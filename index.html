Ad Invader — Privacy Policy and Support

Effective date: 2 October 2026

Privacy

Ad Invader does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising networks and no tracking, and it makes no network connections.

Your scores, coins, unlocks and settings are saved only on your device, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app.

The “ads” in the game are jokes drawn by the game itself. They are not real advertisements, they contain no links, and they send nothing anywhere.

The game has no in-app purchases. Coins are earned only by playing.

Children

Because nothing is collected, nothing is collected from children either.
